Evidence 3 · trading edge by indicator

As a trade, only extreme volatility and cheap valuation pay; price dips alone do not

Math 585 · Midterm · Buying Blood, Not Knives · 7

Speaker: [Member 2]. Trading edge = buy on every trigger day, hold one year, compare with buying on any day; capital use ignored. Single indicators only. For each we tried 4–5 thresholds (e.g. VIX 20/25/30/35/40) and kept the best one that fired in at least 8 separate episodes. Significance: circular-shift test (the trigger series is shifted in time 1,000 times, keeping its clustering). Two families work: extreme implied volatility — VIX ≥ 40 (+37 pp, 51% vs 14%), VXN ≥ 40 (+31 pp), VIX 1.5x spike (+15 pp) — and cheap valuation on 140 years of S&P data — equity risk premium in its top 30% (+7.8 pp), trailing P/E in its bottom 40% (+5.2 pp), CAPE bottom 30% (+4.8 pp); earnings lagged 3 months. Price-only triggers (drawdown, MA200, RSI, realized vol, 5-day drop) LOSE over the full history because the 2000–02 bust fired them for three years. Orange diamonds = same rule in the last five years. Choosing the best of a grid flatters the edge.
