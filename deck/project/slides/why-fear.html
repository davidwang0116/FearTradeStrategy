Why sentiment

Fear is a risk premium you can be paid for

“Be fearful when others are greedy, and be greedy when others are fearful.”

— Warren Buffett, “Buy American. I Am.” New York Times, Oct 16, 2008

Sentiment premium

When investor sentiment is low, subsequent returns are high (Baker & Wurgler, 2006).

Fear gauge

VIX / VXN spikes coincide with forced selling; implied vol overshoots (Whaley, 2000).

Variance risk premium

Implied minus realised variance predicts index returns (Bollerslev, Tauchen & Zhou, 2009).

Valuation

Cheap starting CAPE → higher 10-year returns (Campbell & Shiller, 1998).
