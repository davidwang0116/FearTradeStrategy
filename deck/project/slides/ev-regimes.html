Speaker: [Member 2]. Robustness. Within every sub-period the most-fearful quintile beats the calmest. But across periods it is a Simpson's-paradox story: 2000–02 was fearful for three years while QQQ fell 80%. Fear alone cannot tell a crash bottom from a valuation bust — that's why the dashboard carries a valuation term (35% weight) and why we add a trend guard.
